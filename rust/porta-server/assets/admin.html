New client

One secure token can enroll devices up to your chosen limit.

Leave a limit blank for none. Access ends at the end of the expiry day. Quotas count upload plus download per calendar month (UTC). The speed limit applies to each direction across all of the client's devices.

Third-party RFC 9484 CONNECT-IP clients may connect with this client's token alone. They have no Porta device key, share one device slot and address, and the token is their only credential.

Network access

Comma-separated IPv4 or IPv6 prefixes such as 10.20.0.0/16 or 2001:db8::/32. Blocked destinations win over allowed ones; the gateway and its DNS server stay reachable. Devices of one client reach each other, and clients that share a group reach each other's devices and subnets. Saving a change reconnects the client's devices.
